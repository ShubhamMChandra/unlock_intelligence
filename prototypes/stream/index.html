Two days, in the room

Live, with your team, on your team's real work. Day one is one person's thread. Day two is the team's stream.

Day one: one person's thread
  1. Live demo on real data. The instructor builds an agent on a volunteer's real work.
  2. The room maps that demo across people, tools, trainings, guardrails and metrics.
  3. Each person builds their own, from a starter written for their role.
  4. Read the first output honestly, tighten it, run it again.
  5. Share across the room. Same pattern, very different outputs.
Day two: the team's stream
  1. Pick the process you keep postponing.
  2. Map it end to end: how it runs today, and rebuilt, with the agent steps marked.
  3. Pull out the next three to five builds.
  4. A peer from another role pressure-tests the map.
  5. One named action for Monday.

What changes by Monday

Your team decides what an agent runs and which calls stay with people. Then it builds them.

Is there a corporate or team option?

Yes. For teams of five or more we offer custom scheduling, role-specific curriculum tailoring and volume pricing. Reach out and we'll put together a proposal.

How do you measure outcomes?

Everyone takes a confidence assessment before and after. Your team then receives an executive summary with aggregated results, completion tracking and competency gains you can hand straight to L&D.

Do I need any technical background?

None. The program is designed for people who have never written code and never plan to. If you can use email and a web browser, you have everything you need.

Is this online or in person?

Both. Cohorts run live over video, or at select city venues. Ask us what's available for your team.

We already have Coursera or LinkedIn Learning. Why this?

Those are good reference libraries. This is live and built around exercises your team does together, on its own work, and you leave with a map of one real process and the builds that come next.

Is this affiliated with the University of Chicago?

Unlock Intelligence is an independent program. Our Head of Curriculum teaches AI and entrepreneurship at the University of Chicago and brings that rigor here. The curriculum is original.
